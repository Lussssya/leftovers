# Refrigerator foley

Source: **Fridge door**, sound #1158, by **Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank**.

- Source page: https://bigsoundbank.com/fridge-door-s1158.html
- Download used: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/1158.mp3
- License: **CC0 (public domain)**, as stated on the source page, checked 2026-10-09.

The page permits editing, redistribution, and commercial game use. Attribution
is not required, but retained here to credit the recording's author.

The recording contains five refrigerator openings/closings and compressor
noise. Clips here are edited excerpts, not AI-generated audio. Processing:
mono PCM WAV at 48 kHz, compressor/DC reduction, pre-roll trimming, short fades,
peak adjustment, low-pass softening for gentle closes, crossfade for movement.
The movement layer is an edited handling-noise excerpt, not a continuous
recording of a hinge at every possible speed. Source timings are recorded in
Tools/prepare_fridge_audio.py. No other third-party audio is included.

Fridge_Hum.wav uses the steady compressor sound from 3.8 to 6.5 seconds of the
same recording. Processing: DC removal, 260 Hz low-pass filtering, 120 ms loop
crossfade, and peak normalization to 0.5. The final mono loop lasts 2.58 seconds.
