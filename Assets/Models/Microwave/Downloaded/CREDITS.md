User-supplied model downloads, October 17, 2026.

Microwave: microwave-oven.zip, containing model.dae and the MicrowaveOven texture set. Original body, door, and glass turntable are used. The door was restored from its supplied open pose to a hinged closed pose. The upper dial geometry was removed for the digital timer.

Plate: plates.zip, using the shallow plate mesh from Plates.fbx. The deeper bowl and source scene camera/light are not instantiated.

Sandwich: sandvich.zip, using the complete unbitten sandwich meshes from SANDVICH.glb and its original embedded c_sandwich texture. The bitten variant is excluded.

Geometry was normalized to metres, Y up, microwave front +Z. Source archives and recordings remain unchanged in Downloads. SOURCE_FILES.json records the exact inputs. These are the user-supplied models, replacing the earlier procedural prototype geometry.
