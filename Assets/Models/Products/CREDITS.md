The ketchup, tomato, cheese, egg, and bread models come from Kenney Food Kit 2.0.
Kenney released these models and the color texture under CC0.
The full license is in Kenney-License.txt.

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/kenney_food-kit.zip

The curled lettuce leaf was made for this project. It uses the same color texture.

The mesh assets fit inside the original product colliders. Their lower surfaces
stay at the original cube bottoms. The saved product transforms remain unchanged.

The editable OBJ sources and the preparation script are in Tools/ProductModels.
Run `python3 Tools/ProductModels/prepare_models.py` from the project folder to
rebuild the mesh assets and update the Kitchen scene references.
